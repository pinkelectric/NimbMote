NimbMote

Privacy

NimbMote is designed to stay local.

NimbMote connects your Android phone to your Windows computer over the same local network. It does not require an account.

⌁

What moves between devices

Media title, artist, artwork, playback state and position; Windows volume; the selected wallpaper preview; and the commands you explicitly send from the phone.

⌾

How pairing works

The Windows Agent displays a one-time six-digit code. Enter it in NimbMote while both devices are on the same Wi-Fi. The pairing secret protects later communication between those two devices.

What NimbMote does not do

  • It does not create a NimbMote account.
  • It does not upload your desktop screenshot: the phone receives the configured Windows wallpaper preview only.
  • It does not send audio from the computer to the phone.
  • It does not collect advertising or analytics data.
